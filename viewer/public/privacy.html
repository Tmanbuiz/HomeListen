Home Listen privacy policy

Published by WianTribe Inc. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

What Home Listen is

Home Listen lets an Android phone left at home send sound alerts, and optionally live sound, to phones you have paired with it. It is not an emergency alarm or a safety or medical device.

Audio

  • Listening starts only on the home phone, by the person using it. It cannot be started from a notification, a restart or a remote request. The home phone shows a notification with a Stop button while the microphone is on.
  • In Guardian mode no live audio is created. Sounds are recognised on the home phone itself.
  • In Live + Guardian mode, live sound goes directly to a paired listener over an encrypted connection while you listen.
  • Home Listen does not record, save, upload or keep audio or transcripts.

Information the service handles

  • Alert details: the kind of sound (for example barking or a loud noise), the time, and the name of the room. These are used to send you the alert. The service keeps short-lived delivery records so an alert is not sent twice, and removes them automatically when they expire.
  • Pairing and access: an anonymous account ID, the role you were given (caregiver, alerts only or guest), and expiry times. Pairing links expire after 10 minutes and sign-in codes work once.
  • Notification address: a push token for your phone or browser, used only to deliver alerts to that phone.
  • On your phone: your alert history is stored on the listening phone, not on our servers. You can export or clear it in the app.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or sites.

Camera

The camera is used only on the pairing screen, to scan the QR code shown on your other phone. No pictures are kept.

Who processes it

Home Listen runs on Cloudflare (hosting) and Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in, signalling and push notifications). They process the information above on our behalf. Optional features you turn on yourself, such as Tailscale or Home Assistant, are run by you and governed by their own terms.

Your choices

  • Remove a listening phone in the app and its access ends.
  • Clear the alert history on your phone at any time.
  • To ask about or delete anything we hold, write to the support address below.

Responsible use

Only use Home Listen in a home you are entitled to listen in, and tell the people who live there that it is on.

Children

Home Listen is for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
