Common questions

The pairing link stopped working. Links expire after 10 minutes. Create a new one on the home phone.

I do not get alerts on my iPhone. Open Home Listen from the Home Screen icon and choose Enable alerts. Live listening needs the app open; alerts use notifications.

Live listening is unavailable. The home phone must be in Live + Guardian mode and your access must allow it.

How do I stop listening? Press Stop in the home phone's microphone notification or in the app.

Home Listen is not an emergency alarm. See the privacy policy.
